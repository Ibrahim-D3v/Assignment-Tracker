☕ EduCalm
Built for flow states, not burnout.
EduCalm is a minimalist, cross-platform academic manager designed to replace the sterile, anxiety-inducing blue light of traditional productivity apps with a calming, organic workflow. It blends the warmth of a local coffee shop with the rigorous organization of a university library, helping students track assignments, execute deep-work focus sessions, and visualize their productivity rhythms.

✨ Core Features
Smart Sorting Dashboard: A custom algorithm that intelligently weighs both the actual due date and the priority level to float the most pressing tasks to the top.
Deep-Work Focus Timer: An integrated Pomodoro engine (25-minute blocks) to track deep work, coupled with a micro-task breakdown checklist.
Academic Insights: Visual analytics tracking completion efficiency, total focused hours, and weekly productivity rhythms.
Micro-Task Mapping: Break massive, overwhelming assignments into tiny, actionable sub-tasks that feel impossible to fail.
Local Notifications: Automated 24-hour warning pings for approaching deadlines.

🎨 The "Cozy Earth" Design System
EduCalm rejects traditional harsh shadows and sharp edges in favor of Warm Minimalism.
• Shape Language: Ultra-soft 32px (2rem) pill-shaped containers to make the interface feel approachable and safe.
• Tonal Layering: Zero elevation. Depth is achieved entirely through flat color-blocking and subtle tonal borders.
• Dynamic Theming: Seamless transitions between a rich, coffee-brown Light Mode and a luminous, caramel-tinted Dark Mode to reduce eye strain during late-night study sessions.
• Typography: Powered by Public Sans for institutional clarity and neutral, rhythmic spacing.
